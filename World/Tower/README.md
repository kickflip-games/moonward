# Tower integration

The main scene is [World.tscn](../World.tscn). It instances [IntegratedMap.tscn](../Map/IntegratedMap.tscn), which inherits the original map. The entrance, lower mechanisms, original spike shafts, turrets, springboards, camera framing and summit stay in the continuous climb.

## Redesigned central ascent

[integrated_map.gd](../Map/integrated_map.gd) inserts a 2,304px chapter at y=-4192, immediately before the original scaffold grid. All original terrain cells and mechanisms remain; everything above the insertion moves upward by 2,304px. New stone uses the original TileMap collision tiles and BlackSpire terrain renderer.

| Section | References | Challenge |
|---|---|---|
| Collapsing Stair, y=-4140 to -4800 | 05 Broken Bridge, 08 Collapsing Stair | Four 95px planks, 144px rises, alternating 224–256px transfers. Planks collapse after 0.48s. |
| Counterweight / Relay Lifts, y=-4800 to -5504 | 07 Counterweight Lift, 09 Crank Yard, 15 Relay Lifts | 224px vertical and 320px lateral travel; 112px moving decks; one central stone transfer perch. |
| Needle Court, y=-5504 to -6400 | 06 S-Curve, 14 Needle Threader, 24 Pendulum Court | Alternating wall ledges, 128–160px rises, blades across the gaps, and a collapsing link to a shrine before the original scaffolds. |

Thirteen shrines mark chapter boundaries: the original ten plus three at the new section boundaries. There are no room loading screens or per-platform checkpoints. Missing a transfer can cost progress; the controller and wall-jump abilities are unchanged.

Edit `build_ascent()` for geometry and mechanism placement. Stone coordinates and dimensions must align to the 32px world grid. The `@tool` script builds the additions in the editor and at runtime; generated nodes are rebuilt from this source. Edit the inherited original map for the rest of the course.

## Experimental room library

[PrototypeWorld.tscn](PrototypeWorld.tscn) preserves the rejected 30-room assembly for experiments. It is **not** the main game. Its `Rooms/` scenes and `room_manifest.json` are generated by [build_reference_tower.py](../../Tools/build_reference_tower.py). That command only regenerates the prototype library.

[LegacyWorld.tscn](../LegacyWorld.tscn) preserves the original course for comparison and historical terrain tests.

## Validation

Run `GODOT_BIN='/path/to/Godot' bash Tests/run_checks.sh`. The integrated suite checks safe checkpoints/reloading, the continuous collapsing stair, both live lift transfers, the court with blades active, terrain retention and the original win flow. It also checks every original mechanism survives at its correctly shifted height and tests the connecting jumps onto the original scaffold grid. The prototype's older neutral-platform jump checks are separate.

See [the current review](../../artifacts/integrated_tower/README.md). Scripted traversal is bounded coverage; a human full-tower playthrough is still needed to judge pacing and difficulty.
