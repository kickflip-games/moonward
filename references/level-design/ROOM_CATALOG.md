## Principles

- Teach each new trap individually before combining traps.
- Use warning tells for darts, flames, saws and crushers.
- Give hard rooms safe waiting platforms and quick retry checkpoints.
- Alternate flowing traversal with hard challenges; protect the calm moon-view finale.
