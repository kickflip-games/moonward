# Games to study for Moonward

These are **design references, not assets to reuse**. Track what is relevant to Moonward's fast, readable climb, and distinguish observations from store-page descriptions.

## Tootum

- **Steam:** https://store.steampowered.com/app/3210910/Tootum/
- **Developer:** Douglas Oliveira
- **Why it's on the list:** Pixel-art platformer / Metroidvania reference for a visually cohesive exploratory world. Its Steam description emphasizes secrets, abilities that open new routes, environmental storytelling, and multiple endings.
- **What to study:** How distinct rooms and side routes encourage discovery; how the art separates playable terrain, interactable objects, and backgrounds; whether environmental details/encounters reward curiosity.
- **Moonward adaptation:** Keep the central goal a flowing vertical climb toward a moon-view payoff. Borrow *ideas for room atmosphere, readable exploration, and optional discoveries* without adopting Tootum's roguelike build, farming, or upgrade systems by default.
- **Status:** Reference added from the Steam listing; no hands-on gameplay review has been performed.
